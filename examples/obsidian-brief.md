## Executive summary

The programme aims to improve customer experience, consistency, and reduce repetitive manual work. A weekly change dashboard will be created for executives and regional leaders. Store managers will receive a manager enablement pack before the next regional rollout wave. Customer service communications will include a clear statement on role impact, retraining, and support.
